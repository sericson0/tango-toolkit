The **ocho cortado** — literally an “interrupted ocho” — redirects the movement of an ocho
into a compact change of direction.

## Rock step

<RockStepIntro />

Technically, a rock step is a shift of weight back and forth between two feet. You can also
think of it as a quick step in which you do not fully commit your weight before returning to
the original foot.
