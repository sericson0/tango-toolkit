Your <Term id="axis" /> is the vertical line you balance on. On your own axis you could stand
there all night without your partner. Off it, every step is a small rescue — and your partner
can feel every one of them.

<Callout type="note" title="Still being written">
A fuller treatment is coming, including what to do about the lower back and how the embrace
changes what "upright" means.
</Callout>

<KeyPoints title="The short version">

- Stand tall from the floor up, not by lifting the chest.
- Weight forward of the heels, over the ball of the foot.
- Knees soft, never locked, never bent.
- Shoulders down and back without holding them there.
- Look out, not down. The floor will still be there.

</KeyPoints>
